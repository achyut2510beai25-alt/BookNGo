About BookNGo

Our story, mission and the team behind it all

Our Story

BookNGo was born in 2021 in a small Mumbai apartment where three college friends were frustrated trying to book tickets for a live concert. The process was slow, confusing and stressful. That night they asked themselves — why isn't there a simpler way?


Within months they built the first version of BookNGo — a clean, fast and reliable ticket booking platform. Starting with just 5 events in Mumbai, BookNGo now hosts thousands of events across India covering concerts, sports, movies and theatre.


Today BookNGo serves over 2 million users across 20+ cities and is trusted by top artists, sports teams and cinema chains across the country.
